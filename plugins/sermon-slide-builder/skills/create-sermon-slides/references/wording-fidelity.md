# Wording Fidelity Contract

The sermon note is the authority. Presentation work may clarify the visual
hierarchy, but it must not become an unrequested rewrite of the sermon.

## Slide Boundaries

- Treat each case-insensitive `SL` marker as a requested slide boundary.
- Preserve cue order.
- Default to one slide per cue.
- Permit one extra opener only when the existing series deck already contains
  one or the first cue clearly calls for it.
- Split a cue only for readable Scripture, a long quotation, or a long list.
  Keep the words and order intact across the split.
- Do not merge repeated-looking cues without confirmation; repetition may be
  intentional for pacing or return-to-theme moments.

## Visible Copy

Copy the pastor's words after the marker by default. Preserve:

- theology and doctrinal terms;
- Bible translation and verse wording;
- outline numbers, letters, and hierarchy;
- names, dates, quotations, capitalization used for emphasis, and sequence;
- first-person/second-person voice.

Do not:

- summarize the sermon body into extra slides;
- replace concrete language with marketing copy;
- soften, intensify, modernize, or make a claim more persuasive;
- invent a title, takeaway, statistic, quote, application, or illustration;
- silently correct a factual claim by changing the visible wording.

When a factual claim appears wrong or outdated, flag it for review and supply
the verified evidence separately. Change the slide only after approval.

When a cue gives only a Scripture reference and the deck needs the full passage,
use the translation established in the note, church profile, or series deck. If
none is established, ask the pastor. Record the source passage in the speaker
notes.

## Production Directions

Remove the `SL` token itself. Treat parenthetical phrases such as these as build
instructions:

- `(pic of lake)`
- `(World Cup picture)`
- `Video – great awakening clip`
- `map`
- `theme slide`

Keep meaningful words from the cue. For example, a cue that combines a title
with `pic of lake` still needs the title; only the media direction is excluded
from visible copy.

Use nearby paragraphs to understand the intended image, speaker transition, and
scope. Do not automatically copy them onto the slide.

## Permitted Mechanical Changes

Allow these without altering meaning:

- remove the `SL` marker and separator;
- convert tabs to deliberate line breaks;
- use typographic quotes/dashes consistently;
- wrap a sentence across lines;
- split verbatim content across consecutive slides;
- omit a production direction from the audience-facing canvas.

Record obvious spelling or grammar corrections rather than making them
silently. Never silently change a Scripture quotation.

## Change Ledger

For every non-mechanical change, record:

```text
slide:
source cue:
original:
revised:
reason:
approval:
```

Use one of these approval values:

- `not-needed-verbatim`
- `mechanical-only`
- `pastor-approved`
- `needs-review`

The finished deck must not contain a `needs-review` change unless the handoff
clearly identifies it.

An `EDITED` filename, author metadata, or presence in a church archive can make
a deck useful as visual precedent, but it does not by itself prove that legacy
copy changes were approved. Treat those changes as unverified unless the pastor
confirms them or the approval is documented.
