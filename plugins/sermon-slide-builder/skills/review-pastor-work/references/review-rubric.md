# Pastor Work Review Rubric

## Source fidelity

- Preserve the pastor's wording, theology, Scripture translation, order, and
  explicit `SL` or `Slide` cues.
- Do not promote surrounding sermon prose into slide copy without approval.
- Do not silently correct disputed wording.

## PowerPoint production

- Use the requested 16:9, true 32:9, or exact supplied template dimensions.
- Keep sermon words in an editable text region separate from imagery.
- Keep the established text-left/image-right layout unless the pastor requests
  another layout.
- Never stretch imagery. Check crop, safe zones, overflow, clipping, contrast,
  font substitution, notes, masters, and editability.
- Treat tracked `IMAGE NEEDED` placeholders as draft items, not finished art.

## Images and research

- Route real people, events, documents, products, news, maps, and claimed
  historic locations to authentic sourced media.
- Label debated sites with a visible editable qualifier when the image would
  otherwise imply certainty.
- Keep recurring biblical characters consistent within one sermon.
- Check hands, faces, anatomy, era, clothing, props, fake writing, watermarks,
  modern objects, fantasy effects, and misleading realism.
- Confirm sources, usage rights, AI notes, prompts, continuity records, and edit
  history are present where required.

## Personal rules

- Apply only approved, relevant personal rules.
- Flag a personal rule that conflicts with a higher safety or fidelity rule.
- Do not infer a new preference from one output or save review findings as
  memory.

## Native verification

Render or open the final file in PowerPoint when available. Review every slide
at presentation size. Reopen the saved copy and confirm it remains editable.
