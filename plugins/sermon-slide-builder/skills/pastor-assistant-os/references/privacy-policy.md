# Local Memory Privacy Policy

## Store only

- a short generalized rule;
- a broad category such as layout, imagery, workflow, wording, or privacy;
- a short generalized reason;
- approval date, rule ID, status, and repeat count.

## Never store

- full sermon text, quotations, outlines, titles, or Scripture excerpts;
- counseling, confession, prayer, health, financial, or family details;
- information about minors;
- member, missionary, staff, or donor names;
- email addresses, phone numbers, credentials, tokens, or account details;
- private URLs, local paths, images, audio, video, or presentation files;
- generated character reference images or source photographs.

Keep working sermon assets in a pastor-approved project folder, not in memory.
The per-sermon character bible stays with that sermon and is not carried into a
new sermon unless the pastor explicitly requests reuse.

## Location and sharing

Use the operating system's local application-data folder by default. Do not use
a Git repository, shared church drive, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or
another sync service unless the pastor understands and approves that location.

Do not upload the local OS folder for support. Share a redacted `doctor` result
that contains statuses and counts, not rules or personal paths.
