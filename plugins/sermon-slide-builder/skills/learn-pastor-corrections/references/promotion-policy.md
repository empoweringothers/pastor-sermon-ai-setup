# Correction Promotion Policy

## Good personal rules

A personal rule is reusable, specific enough to test, and independent of one
sermon. Examples:

- Keep content text on the left and its picture in a separate right frame.
- When Peter appears more than once in a sermon, reuse the approved Peter
  reference and locked character description.
- Do not create a new image for numbered supporting subpoints.

## Do not save

- “Make this slide darker.” It may be a one-time art direction.
- sermon quotations, titles, names, dates, or private facts;
- character details that belong only to the current sermon;
- a rule that weakens authenticity, privacy, rights, or sermon fidelity;
- a proposed correction that has not been verified on the current work.

## Duplicates and conflicts

If the rule already exists, use the exact approved wording and record another
occurrence only after the same save consent. If it conflicts with a stored rule,
show one conflict and ask which rule should remain. Do not silently replace it.

## Church-wide proposals

Recommend a proposal after the same approved correction occurs twice, or after
one costly privacy, factual, theological-fidelity, or production failure.
Proposals must be generalized and contain no pastor or sermon identifiers. A
church administrator must independently review and test the proposal before it
can change the public plugin.
